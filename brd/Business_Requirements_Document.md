# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Name

E-Commerce Product Analytics Project

---

## Background

The organization requires better visibility into sales performance, customer behavior, payment methods, and product performance to support business decision-making. Existing reports provide limited insights, making it difficult to identify revenue opportunities and operational challenges.

---

## Business Problem

The business faces several challenges:

- Inconsistent revenue growth
- Limited visibility into product performance
- Delivery delays
- Low customer retention
- Difficulty identifying high-performing product categories

---

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Evaluate payment trends
- Generate actionable business recommendations

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- SQL Data Analysis
- Power BI Dashboard
- Customer Analysis
- Product Analysis
- Revenue Analysis
- Payment Analysis

### Out of Scope

- Machine Learning
- Sales Forecasting
- Inventory Prediction
- Real-time Data Processing

---

## Deliverables

- SQL Scripts
- Power BI Dashboards
- Business Questions
- Business Insights
- Product Performance Analysis
- Business Recommendations

---

## Success Criteria

- Identify key revenue drivers
- Highlight high-performing categories
- Improve visibility into business performance
- Provide actionable recommendations
