# E-Commerce Product Analytics Case Study

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset to uncover business insights related to revenue, customer behavior, payment methods, delivery performance, and product performance.

Using SQL and Power BI, the project identifies business opportunities and provides recommendations to improve operational efficiency and revenue growth.

---

# Business Challenge

The organization faced multiple business challenges:

- Fluctuating monthly revenue
- Limited visibility into customer behavior
- Lack of product performance insights
- Delivery delays
- Difficulty identifying high-performing categories

---

# Objectives

The project was designed to:

- Analyze business performance
- Evaluate customer purchasing behavior
- Measure product category performance
- Build interactive dashboards
- Generate actionable recommendations

---

# Dataset

Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (Olist)

Tables Used:

- Customers

- Sellers

# Key Insights

- Revenue is concentrated in a limited number of product categories.
- Credit Cards dominate customer payments.
- Product performance varies significantly across categories.
- Seasonal trends influence revenue.
- Customer purchasing behavior indicates opportunities for retention initiatives.

---

# Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing product categories.
- Launch targeted promotions for underperforming products.
- Improve customer retention through loyalty programs.
- Optimize marketing during peak sales periods.
- Monitor product performance continuously through dashboards.
