# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset to evaluate sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, payment trends, and product performance. The goal is to identify business opportunities and provide actionable recommendations for improving revenue and customer satisfaction.

---

# Sales Performance

### Findings

- Revenue demonstrates noticeable fluctuations across different months.
- A small number of product categories contribute a large share of overall revenue.
- Premium-priced products generate significant revenue despite comparatively lower order volumes.

### Recommendation

- Increase inventory for consistently high-performing categories.
- Launch seasonal marketing campaigns during historically high-performing months.
- Monitor declining categories and adjust promotional strategies accordingly.

---

# Customer Insights

### Findings

- Most customers fall into the Low Value customer segment.
- Customer purchasing behavior indicates limited repeat purchases.

### Recommendation

- Introduce customer loyalty programs.
- Offer personalized discounts to encourage repeat purchases.
- Implement customer retention campaigns.

---

# Product Performance

### Findings

- Beauty and Home-related categories are among the strongest revenue generators.
- Several product categories contribute only a small percentage of overall revenue.
- Revenue distribution is concentrated within a limited number of categories.

### Recommendation

- Expand inventory within high-performing categories.
- Review pricing and marketing strategies for underperforming categories.
- Optimize product assortment based on revenue contribution.

---

# Payment Analysis

### Findings

- Credit Card is the most frequently used payment method.
- Other payment methods represent a relatively small share of transactions.

### Recommendation

- Continue optimizing the Credit Card payment experience.
- Promote alternative payment methods through targeted incentives where appropriate.

---

# Business Impact

Implementing these recommendations could help:

- Increase revenue
- Improve customer retention
- Optimize inventory management
- Improve marketing effectiveness
- Support data-driven product decisions

---

# Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be used together to transform raw e-commerce data into actionable business insights. The dashboards and supporting analysis provide decision-makers with valuable information for improving business performance and product strategy.
