# 📊 E-Commerce Product Analytics Project

An end-to-end analytics project built using the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset to uncover business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, payment methods, and product performance. The project combines SQL for data analysis, Power BI for interactive dashboards, and Business Analysis techniques to generate actionable recommendations.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes real-world e-commerce data to identify key business trends, evaluate product performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

The analysis is divided into two major modules:

- Business Analytics
- Product Performance Analytics

Together, these modules provide a comprehensive understanding of how the business performs across customers, products, revenue, and payment behavior.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The organization faces several business challenges:

- Inconsistent revenue growth
- Delivery delays
- Declining customer retention
- Limited visibility into product performance
- Lack of insights for category-level decision making

This project aims to identify the root causes behind these challenges and provide actionable business recommendations using data analytics.

---

- Analyze overall sales performance

# 💡 Key Business Insights

### Business Analytics

- Revenue shows noticeable monthly fluctuations.
- Credit Cards account for the majority of customer payments.
- Most customers belong to the Low Value customer segment.
- Revenue is concentrated among a limited number of customers.

### Product Analytics

- Beauty and Home-related categories generate the highest revenue.
- A small number of product categories contribute a significant portion of total revenue.
- Some products generate high revenue despite fewer orders, indicating premium pricing.
- Monthly trends reveal seasonal demand across product categories.

---

# 📊 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can help improve business performance:

- Increase inventory for high-performing product categories.
- Promote underperforming categories using targeted marketing campaigns.
- Focus marketing efforts during peak revenue months.
- Introduce customer loyalty and retention programs.
- Optimize product assortment based on revenue contribution.
- Monitor category performance regularly using interactive dashboards.
